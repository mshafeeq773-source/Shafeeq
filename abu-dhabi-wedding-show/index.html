About

This is Abu Dhabi

One Destination, Extraordinary Possibilities.

The Abu Dhabi Wedding Show brings the global weddings and celebrations community together to share expertise, spark new ideas and explore the possibilities of Abu Dhabi.

The fourth edition of the show welcomes wedding planners, designers, luxury suppliers, creative partners and industry media from the UAE and around the world.

With keynote sessions, panel discussions and networking opportunities, attendees can discover fresh perspectives on the evolving art and business of celebrations, and meet the people who can help bring your next vision to life.

Beyond the conference, experience Abu Dhabi’s distinctive venues, rich cultural heritage and warm Emirati hospitality. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, discover a destination that offers extraordinary possibilities for every wedding story.

Connect

Meet the people behind the possibilities

Connect with industry leaders, creative talent and potential partners from across the global wedding community. Exchange perspectives, explore opportunities for collaboration and build relationships that continue well beyond the show.

Discover

Fresh ideas for your next celebration

Explore new approaches to wedding design, guest experiences and the business of celebrations. Hear from industry voices, discover the expertise of local and international partners, and see how Abu Dhabi can bring a different dimension to your next wedding or celebration.

Experience

Discover Abu Dhabi through its hospitality

Experience the settings, flavours and welcome that make Abu Dhabi distinctive. From gala evenings to destination discovery experiences, explore the emirate’s cultural character, natural beauty and exceptional venues, and imagine how they could become part of your clients’ celebrations.
