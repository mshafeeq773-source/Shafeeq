Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we do with the details you give us when you register for the Abu Dhabi Wedding Show.

Who we are

Registration for the Abu Dhabi Wedding Show is managed by Inspiratus Events together with the Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi (DCT Abu Dhabi). In this policy, “we” means both.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the registration form: your name, work email, mobile number, country of residence, organisation, job title, industry, and any dietary requirements or food allergies you choose to tell us about.

How we use your details

We use your email address only to:

  • contact you about the Abu Dhabi Wedding Show, including your registration and attendance
  • invite you to future events of DCT Abu Dhabi

The rest of your details are used only to manage your registration and your attendance at the Show, for example your dietary requirements for catering. They are not used for any other purpose.

Who may contact you

Inspiratus Events and DCT Abu Dhabi may contact you for the purposes above. By registering, you agree to be contacted by them.

Your choices

If you no longer wish to receive invitations to future events, or you would like us to update or delete your details, email adws@inspirat.us.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your details, email adws@inspirat.us.
