2025 EDITION

Abu Dhabi Wedding Show 2025

  • Four Seasons Hotel Abu Dhabi at Al Maryah Island
  • 15–16 December 2025

Fresh perspectives

on extraordinary celebrations.

The third edition of the Abu Dhabi Wedding Show brought international and regional wedding professionals together to explore the changing world of luxury celebrations, with Abu Dhabi’s cosmopolitan character providing the backdrop.

Hosted at Four Seasons Hotel Abu Dhabi at Al Maryah Island, the conference explored everything from craftsmanship and immersive design to wellness weddings, business growth and the creative potential of AI. Industry experts shared perspectives on creating deeply personal experiences and finding inspiration in unexpected venues.

The evening continued with a gala dinner and awards ceremony at the W Abu Dhabi – Yas Island, celebrating the industry in a setting overlooking the iconic Yas Marina racetrack.

On the second day, curated discovery trips introduced guests to Abu Dhabi’s diverse venues and experiences, from teamLab Phenomena to island retreats and waterfront hotels. Together, the programme offered a closer look at the emirate’s versatility and fresh inspiration for future destination weddings.
