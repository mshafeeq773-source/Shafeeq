About

This is Abu Dhabi

One Destination,
Extraordinary Possibilities.

The Abu Dhabi Wedding Show brings the global weddings and celebrations community together to share expertise, spark new ideas and explore the possibilities of Abu Dhabi.

The fourth edition of the show welcomes wedding planners, designers, luxury suppliers, creative partners and industry media from the UAE and around the world.

With keynote sessions, panel discussions and networking opportunities, attendees can discover fresh perspectives on the evolving art and business of celebrations, and meet the people who can help bring your next vision to life.

Beyond the conference, experience Abu Dhabi’s distinctive venues, rich cultural heritage and warm Emirati hospitality. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, discover a destination that offers extraordinary possibilities for every wedding story.
